Anittas text: Pithānas, king of Kussara, took the city of Nesa in a night raid. "Did no evil" to its subjects. Anittas, son of Pithānas, went to war with King Huzziyas of Zālpuwas to recover "our god". Captured god, city, and king. Took Hattusas in a night raid and cursed it. (Burned?)

Old Kingdom starts with "Labarnas" (Tabarnas?) who might be Hattusilis I. Labarnas inherited from father-in-law Hesmi-Sarrumas; civil war against Hesmi-Sarruma's son Papahdilmah. Labarnas extinguished the families of all his enemies and expanded into Arzawa.

Hattusilis I, son of the brother of Tawannannas, moved the capital to Hattusas and expanded south into Syria. Erased the names of Tawannannas and her sons. Expanded to Wilusa northwest and Alalah southeast.

Mursilis I, grandson of Hattusilis I, destroyed the kingdom of Yamhad to extend borders to the sea. Sacked Babylon, ending the Amorites (Hammurabi) and letting Kassites take over. Intended heir was his son Pisenis.

Mursilis assassinated by brother-in-law/cupbearer Hantilis I and son-in-law Zidantas; killed Pisenis and his children and took power. Hantilis rebuilt the walls of Hattusas and returned a stolen statue of Marduk to Babylon in exchange for a peace treaty. Focused on Hurrians to the east and neglected northern borders, letting Kaskans destroy Nerik. Died comfortably of old age.

Zidantas did nothing and got assassinated by his son Ammunas, the Axe King. Ammunas lost Arzawa (Luwia), Pala, and Kizzuwatna (Adaniya/Cilicia) in a civil war. Ammunas's son Huzziyas I killed his brothers to inherit. Huzziyas's brother-in-law Telipinus deposed but didn't kill him. Telipinus allied with Kizzuwatna against Mitanni, wrote succession laws, then died.

Middle Kingdom is a Dark Age. Capital moved to Sapinuwa then Samuha. Tudhaliyas I reconquered Assuwa and Arzawa, defeated Aleppo and Mitanni. Some time after him, enemies (Kaskans?) razed Hattusas.

New Kingdom starts with Suppiluliumas I, who took back territory and portrayed himself as a divine ruler (instead of first among equals). Incited revolts against Akhenaten, almost solved things with a marriage alliance, but his son Zannanzas died suspiciously. Blamed Pharaoh Ay and declared war on Egypt. Great conquests, but plague brought back from Egypt devastated Hattusas, killed Suppiluliumas I and successor Arnuwandas II.

Arnuwandas's brother Mursilis II took power, young, seen as weak. (Called a child, but couldn't be by succession laws.) Put down Kaskan rebellions and reconquered lots of land. Solar eclipse was an omen.

Gradual decline after Mursilis II, eventually fell in the Bronze Age Collapse when Hattusas was razed.
