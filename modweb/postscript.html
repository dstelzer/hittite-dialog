Congratulations on finishing the game!

Have you learned the Hittite names for the compass directions? (Try looking at a nearby room.) These were only discovered in 2023! (Kreitzscheck, "The Hittite words for North, East, South, and West")

There are two ways to deal with the baker in tablet 2, one violent, one non-violent. Both of them involve the door bolt from tablet 1.

The puzzle on tablet 3 is randomly generated every time you restart the game, with over ten thousand possibilities.

Hittite has a very simple noun gender system: all nouns are either "common" or "neuter". This game doesn't put much focus on it, but the nouns that look the same in the nominative and accusative are neuter, and the ones that don't are common. (Neuter nouns in Hittite actually don't have a nominative or accusative at all, just a merged case called the "absolutive".)
